Title

Dialog 4

Subtitle

 A Small Treat

Learning focus

Asking whether someone wants something, choosing between simple food items, and expressing a different choice.

Scene summary

After deciding what they will drink, Narin asks Mali if she would like a snack. Mali agrees. Narin asks which snack she wants, and she chooses cake. Mali then asks whether Narin will also take cake. He answers that he will take ice cream instead.

Register

Polite, friendly adult conversation

Blocks

Block 1
Thai: นริน: เอาขนมไหมครับ
Transliteration: Narin: ao kà-nǒm mǎi kráp
English: Narin: Would you like a snack?

Block 2
Thai: มะลิ: เอาค่ะ
Transliteration: Mali: ao kâ
English: Mali: Yes, I will.

Block 3

Transliteration: Narin: ao kà-nǒm à-rai kráp

Block 4
