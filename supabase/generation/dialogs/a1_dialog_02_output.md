Title

Dialog 2

Subtitle

Where are you going?

Learning focus

Ask where someone is going, invite them to do something together, and accept an invitation.

Scene summary

Continuation of the first, polite introduction between Mali and Narin in an everyday setting. Narin asks where she is going and invites her for coffee.

Register

polite

English translation

Narin: Where are you going?
Mali: I am going to drink coffee.
Narin: Would you like to drink coffee together?
Mali: Yes, I'd like to.
Mali: Let's go together.
Narin: Okay.
