Title

Dialog 5

Subtitle

Enjoying the Food

Learning focus

Express likes, describe food, and talk about eating habits using simple adverbs.

Scene summary

Their drinks and snacks have arrived. Mali and Narin taste the food, talk about whether they like it, describe it as delicious and sweet, and briefly talk about how often they eat snacks.

Register

Polite, friendly adult conversation

Blocks

Block 1
Thai: มะลิ: เค้กอร่อยไหมคะ
Transliteration: Mali: kéek à-rɔ̀i mǎi ká
English: Mali: Is the cake delicious?

Block 2
Thai: นริน: อร่อยมากครับ
Transliteration: Narin: à-rɔ̀i mâak kráp
English: Narin: It's very delicious.

Block 3
Thai: นริน: ชอบเค้กไหมครับ
Transliteration: Narin: chɔ̂ɔp kéek mǎi kráp
English: Narin: Do you like cake?

Block 4
Thai: มะลิ: ชอบค่ะ เค้กหวานค่ะ
Transliteration: Mali: chɔ̂ɔp kâ. kéek wǎan kâ.
English: Mali: I do. The cake is sweet.

Block 5
Thai: มะลิ: ชอบไอศกรีมไหมคะ
Transliteration: Mali: chɔ̂ɔp ai-sà-griim mǎi ká
English: Mali: Do you like ice cream?

Block 6
Thai: นริน: ชอบครับ
Transliteration: Narin: chɔ̂ɔp kráp
English: Narin: I do.

Block 7
Thai: มะลิ: กินขนมบ่อยไหมคะ
Transliteration: Mali: gin kà-nǒm bɔ̀i mǎi ká
English: Mali: Do you often eat snacks?

Block 8
Thai: นริน: ไม่กินขนมบ่อยครับ
Transliteration: Narin: mâi gin kà-nǒm bɔ̀i kráp
English: Narin: I don't eat snacks often.
