Title

Dialog 5

Subtitle

Enjoying the Food

Learning focus

Express likes, describe food, and talk about eating habits using simple adverbs.

Scene summary

Their drinks and snacks have arrived. Mali and Narin taste the food, talk about whether they like it, describe it as delicious and sweet, and briefly talk about how often they eat snacks.

Register

Polite, friendly adult conversation

Blocks

Blocks

Block 1
Thai: นริน: เค้กอร่อยไหมครับ
Transliteration: Narin: kéek à-rɔ̀i mǎi kráp
English: Narin: Is the cake delicious?

Block 2
Thai: มะลิ: อร่อยค่ะ เค้กหวาน ชอบขนมหวานค่ะ
Transliteration: Mali: à-rɔ̀i kâ. kéek wǎan. chɔ̂ɔp kà-nǒm wǎan kâ.
English: Mali: It is delicious. The cake is sweet. I like sweet snacks.

Block 3
Thai: มะลิ: ชอบเค้กด้วยไหมคะ
Transliteration: Mali: chɔ̂ɔp kéek dûai mǎi ká
English: Mali: Do you like cake too?

Block 4
Thai: นริน: ชอบครับ คุณชอบไอศกรีมด้วยไหมครับ
Transliteration: Narin: chɔ̂ɔp kráp. kun chɔ̂ɔp ai-sà-griim dûai mǎi kráp
English: Narin: I do. Do you like ice cream too?

Block 5
Thai: มะลิ: ชอบค่ะ กินขนมบ่อยไหมคะ
Transliteration: Mali: chɔ̂ɔp kâ. gin kà-nǒm bɔ̀i mǎi ká
English: Mali: I do. Do you often eat snacks?

Block 6
Thai: นริน: ไม่กินขนมบ่อยครับ
Transliteration: Narin: mâi gin kà-nǒm bɔ̀i kráp
English: Narin: I don't eat snacks often.
