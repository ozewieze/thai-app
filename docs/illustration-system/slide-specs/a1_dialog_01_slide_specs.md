# Slide Specifications — a1_dialog_01

Bron: `dialog_blocks` van de dialoog gekoppeld aan `lesson_key = 'a1-dialog-01'` (7 blokken, `block_index` 0–6, uit `seed-data/dialogs/a1_dialog_01.seed.sql`). Er bestaan nog geen rijen in `dialog_slides` voor deze dialoog — deze specificatie is de basis om ze aan te maken (insert, geen update).

## Segmentatie-overzicht

| Slide | Blokken (`block_index`) | Gespreksmoment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–1 | Wederzijdse begroeting |
| 2 | 2–3 | Mali stelt zich voor en vraagt Narins naam |
| 3 | 4–6 | Narin stelt zich voor; wederzijds "aangenaam kennis te maken" (afsluiting) |

**Segmentatie-motivatie:** 3 slides voor een dialoog van 7 blokken, geen slide met meer dan 3 blokken, elke knip valt op een natuurlijke wisseling van gespreksmoment (groet → introductie/vraag → afsluiting), conform de heuristiek in `04_illustration_workflow_guide.md`.

## Slide 1

### Herkomst
- `dialog_slides.slide_index`: `0`
- Blokken: `0`–`1`
- Tekst:
  - มะลิ: สวัสดีค่ะ — "Mali: Hello."
  - นริน: สวัสดีครับ — "Narin: Hello."

### Moment in Dialogue

```
Dialogue Stage
Opening mutual greeting.

Narrative Moment
Mali and Narin greet each other for the first time.

Interaction
Mali speaks first with a polite greeting; Narin returns the greeting
immediately after.

Body Language
Comfortable conversational distance; open, upright posture from both;
slight forward inclination as a polite greeting cue.

Facial Expressions
Warm, polite smiles from both characters.

Conversation Energy
Calm, respectful opening energy — first contact between two adults.

Educational Focus
The learner immediately sees a mutual Thai greeting exchange (สวัสดี)
between two adults meeting for the first time.
```

## Slide 2

### Herkomst
- `dialog_slides.slide_index`: `1`
- Blokken: `2`–`3`
- Tekst:
  - มะลิ: ฉันชื่อมะลิค่ะ — "Mali: My name is Mali."
  - มะลิ: คุณชื่ออะไรคะ — "Mali: What is your name?"

### Moment in Dialogue

```
Dialogue Stage
Self-introduction and name question.

Narrative Moment
Mali states her own name and then asks Narin for his.

Interaction
Mali gestures gently toward herself while introducing her name, then tilts
her head slightly in question toward Narin.

Body Language
Mali relaxed and attentive; Narin listening with polite, engaged attention.

Facial Expressions
Mali friendly and slightly curious; Narin attentive and open.

Conversation Energy
Continuing calm energy, becoming slightly more personal.

Educational Focus
The learner sees a self-introduction pattern (ฉันชื่อ...) immediately
followed by a name question (คุณชื่ออะไร).
```

---

## Slide 3

### Herkomst
- `dialog_slides.slide_index`: `2`
- Blokken: `4`–`6`
- Tekst:
  - นริน: ผมชื่อนรินครับ — "Narin: My name is Narin."
  - นริน: ยินดีที่ได้รู้จักครับ — "Narin: Nice to meet you."
  - มะลิ: ยินดีที่ได้รู้จักค่ะ — "Mali: Nice to meet you."

### Moment in Dialogue

```
Dialogue Stage
Reciprocal introduction and closing.

Narrative Moment
Narin gives his name and closes with "nice to meet you"; Mali echoes the
same closing phrase back.

Interaction
Narin gestures lightly toward himself when naming himself, then gives a
small respectful nod while saying the closing phrase; Mali responds with a
matching, warm acknowledgment.

Body Language
Mutual acknowledgment; small nods from both; posture relaxes slightly
compared to slide 1, signaling a successfully completed first meeting.

Facial Expressions
Both characters smiling, warm and satisfied closing expression.

Conversation Energy
Warm resolution — the first-meeting exchange lands successfully for both.

Educational Focus
The learner sees the reciprocal "nice to meet you" (ยินดีที่ได้รู้จัก)
exchange that conventionally closes a first meeting in Thai.
```
