# Slide Specifications — a1_dialog_02

Bron: `dialog_blocks` van de dialoog gekoppeld aan `lesson_key = 'a1-dialog-02'` (6 blokken, `block_index` 0–5, uit `seed-data/dialogs/a1_dialog_02.seed.sql`). Er bestaan nog geen rijen in `dialog_slides` voor deze dialoog — deze specificatie is de basis om ze aan te maken (insert, geen update).

## Segmentatie-overzicht

| Slide | Blokken (`block_index`) | Gespreksmoment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–1 | Narin vraagt waar Mali heen gaat; zij antwoordt |
| 2 | 2–3 | Narin nodigt haar uit om samen koffie te drinken; zij accepteert |
| 3 | 4–5 | Mali bevestigt dat ze samen gaan; Narin stemt in (afsluiting) |

**Segmentatie-motivatie:** 3 slides voor een dialoog van 6 blokken, elke slide precies 2 blokken (één gespreksuitwisseling per slide), elke knip valt op een natuurlijke wisseling van gespreksmoment (vraag/antwoord → uitnodiging/acceptatie → bevestiging/afsluiting), conform de heuristiek in `04_illustration_workflow_guide.md`.

## Slide 1

### Herkomst
- `dialog_slides.slide_index`: `0`
- Blokken: `0`–`1`
- Tekst:
  - นริน: ไปที่ไหนครับ — "Narin: Where are you going?"
  - มะลิ: ไปดื่มกาแฟค่ะ — "Mali: I am going to drink coffee."

### Moment in Dialogue

```
Dialogue Stage
Casual question about destination.

Narrative Moment
Narin asks Mali where she is headed; she answers that she is going to
drink coffee.

Interaction
Narin turns toward Mali with a light, curious question; Mali answers
easily, perhaps with a small gesture toward the direction she means.

Body Language
Relaxed, upright postures, comfortable conversational distance — a natural
continuation of their earlier first meeting, now more at ease with each
other.

Facial Expressions
Narin friendly and curious; Mali casual and open.

Conversation Energy
Light, easy-going continuation — comfortable small talk between two people
who have just met.

Educational Focus
The learner sees the question pattern ไปที่ไหน ("where are you going")
immediately followed by the answer pattern ไปดื่มกาแฟ ("going to drink
coffee").
```

## Slide 2

### Herkomst
- `dialog_slides.slide_index`: `1`
- Blokken: `2`–`3`
- Tekst:
  - นริน: ดื่มกาแฟด้วยกันไหมครับ — "Narin: Would you like to drink coffee together?"
  - มะลิ: ได้ค่ะ — "Mali: Yes, I'd like to."

### Moment in Dialogue

```
Dialogue Stage
Invitation and acceptance.

Narrative Moment
Narin invites Mali to drink coffee together; she accepts.

Interaction
Narin makes a small, open, inviting gesture (e.g. an open hand toward a
shared direction); Mali responds with a warm nod or small smile of
agreement.

Body Language
Narin slightly leaning in, open and hopeful posture; Mali receptive,
relaxed, nodding in agreement.

Facial Expressions
Narin hopeful and friendly; Mali pleasantly agreeable, a small warm smile.

Conversation Energy
Rising warmth — the invitation makes the exchange a little more personal,
still fully polite.

Educational Focus
The learner sees the invitation pattern ...ด้วยกันไหม ("would you like to
... together") immediately followed by the simple acceptance ได้ค่ะ
("yes, I'd like to").
```

## Slide 3

### Herkomst
- `dialog_slides.slide_index`: `2`
- Blokken: `4`–`5`
- Tekst:
  - มะลิ: ไปด้วยกันค่ะ — "Mali: Let's go together."
  - นริน: ครับ — "Narin: Okay."

### Moment in Dialogue

```
Dialogue Stage
Confirmation and departure.

Narrative Moment
Mali confirms they will go together; Narin gives a simple, agreeable
closing response.

Interaction
Mali gestures toward the direction they will walk, beginning to turn to
go; Narin nods and falls in step beside her.

Body Language
Both figures oriented in the same direction, relaxed synchronized posture
suggesting they are about to walk off together; comfortable, easy
proximity.

Facial Expressions
Both smiling, content and at ease.

Conversation Energy
Warm resolution — the exchange closes on easy companionship as they set
off together.

Educational Focus
The learner sees the closing confirmation ไปด้วยกัน ("let's go together")
paired with the simple agreement ครับ ("okay").
```
