## Scene Bible

```
Location
Same café table as Dialogue 4

Time of day
Daytime, soft natural light.

Weather
Not visually emphasized — no strong weather cue needed for an indoor first
meeting.

Environment
Minimal furniture (a chair, a small table, perhaps a plant), softly
detailed. A richer selection of Thai decorative touches is welcome — for
example patterned cushions or textile details, warm teak or rattan wood
tones, a small tabletop fountain, framed Thai-style artwork or botanical
prints, or decorative Thai ceramics — as long as it reads as a considered,
contemporary interior rather than a cluttered souvenir shop. Avoid literal
shop signage, tourist-market clichés, religious imagery (e.g. Buddha
statues, spirit houses, or altars), or visual clutter that would date or
over-localize the scene.

Unlike Dialog 3/4, where the table was deliberately empty (nothing ordered
yet), the table in Dialog 5 already holds the just-served order — present
and unchanged across all three slides of this dialogue:
- Mali: an iced coffee in a tall glass (coffee, ice cubes, a swirl of
  crème fraîche/whipped cream on top, a straw) and a slice of cake on a
  small plate.
- Narin: a cup/glass of tea and a bowl or cup of ice cream.
No one orders, waits for, or gestures toward the other person's items —
each stays with their own.

Mood
Polite, calm, companionable ease — no longer a first meeting (they already
met and agreed to get coffee together in Dialog 2), but still measured and
appropriately restrained rather than familiar or intimate.

Visual Density
Clean and uncluttered unless specified otherwise.

Color Palette
Warm off-whites, muted warm neutrals, soft sand tones. Restrained saturation.
```
